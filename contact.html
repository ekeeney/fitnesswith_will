Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get started?

Simply reach out through this contact form or call me directly. We'll schedule a free consultation to discuss your goals, fitness level, and how I can help you succeed.

Do you offer online coaching?

Yes! I offer both online and in-person training. Online coaching includes personalized programming, form videos, and regular check-ins via video call.

What if I'm a beginner?

Perfect! I work with clients of all fitness levels. I'll customize a program that's challenging yet achievable, and ensure proper form and progression.

How often should I train?

This depends on your goals and current fitness level. Most clients benefit from 3-5 sessions per week, but we'll determine what's best for you during our consultation.

Do you help with nutrition?

I provide general nutrition guidance and help create sustainable eating habits. For specialized dieting, I may recommend working with a registered dietitian.

What are your rates?

Pricing varies based on the package you choose. I offer hourly sessions, packages, and monthly memberships. Contact me for detailed pricing information.
