Meet Will

The Journey

Will's journey into fitness started through sports like baseball, wrestling, and track, where he learned discipline, consistency, and hard work from a young age. These formative years taught him that physical strength is built through dedication, focus, and pushing past limits.

A turning point came when Will suffered a torn shoulder labrum. What could have been the end of his athletic career became the beginning of something bigger. Through rehabilitation, training, and mental resilience, Will not only recovered but came out stronger — both physically and mentally. This experience transformed his understanding of fitness from just performance to true health and personal transformation.

Philosophy

Will believes that fitness is a journey, not a destination. His approach combines proven training science with genuine care for each client's personal progress. Whether you're brand new to the gym or looking to take your training to the next level, Will creates a supportive and motivating environment where you can build confidence, improve your health, and enjoy the process of becoming stronger every day.
